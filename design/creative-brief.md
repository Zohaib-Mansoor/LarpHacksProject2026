# PlayCall product pass

Latest user direction: substantially less text, a Wise-inspired landing page, a Google Meet-inspired calling experience, server-assigned roles and negotiation goals, a leaderboard, account page, login restricted to ages 13+, privacy policy and terms. Push the completed work to Zohaib-Mansoor/LarpHacksProject2026.

## Primary job
Start a five-minute negotiation with a friend, understand a private role and goal, and inspect a mutually confirmed result. Supporting information is disclosed when requested.

## Direction
One bold, compact hero and one primary action; blue/white PlayCall identity with a warm partner accent. Wise informs the short proposition and confident composition, not its financial claims or brand assets. Google Meet informs the large two-person call stage, quiet top bar, and compact bottom controls, not cameras. Apple guidance informs grouped controls, readable system typography and mobile sheets.

## Non-negotiables
- No cameras, fake online counts, fake scores or invented live coaching.
- Three private mission goals stay server-assigned; only mission points determine standings.
- A collapsible dossier and optional captions/chat keep the stage quiet.
- Both confirmations refer to the same offer revision; edits invalidate acceptance.
- Login and account creation enforce a server-side age gate. A self-declaration is not identity/age verification.
- Public leaderboard participation is opt-in and excludes personal/coaching/transcript data.
- Actual voice requires a configured relay; disclose unavailable infrastructure honestly.
- Preserve teammates' database files and npm commands when integrating main.

## Tokens
Paper #f6f7fa, surface #ffffff, ink #15233b, muted #5c667a, blue #2456db, warm partner #aa481c. Use semantic tokens, system sans, 16–17px body, 44px minimum interactive targets, 8/16/24/48 spacing. Dark appearance and reduced motion remain supported.

## Content budget
Landing first viewport: one title, one short sentence, two actions and compact navigation. Call stage: participant names/roles, timer, connection status and controls. All longer rules, goals, transcript and infrastructure notes live in drawers, forms or legal pages.
